FCC Ads — Business Monitoring
Oct 2026  ·  Scope: Business and reporting RCA for FCC and Client (Nahdi) — technical RCA is out of scope of this document.
The One Number
Ad Revenue
The top-line outcome of the ads business. Ad Revenue = f(Supply, Demand) — it is bounded by whichever side is the limiting constraint. High supply with low demand leaves inventory unfilled. High demand with low supply means advertisers can't reach audiences. Growth requires both sides to scale together.
Total ad revenue — Nahdi platform Total ad revenue — FCC view WoW / MoM trend Revenue per 1,000 ad requests (RPM)
Ad Revenue = min(Supply, Demand)
A drop in Ad Revenue means either supply has contracted, demand has weakened, or both. The sections below diagnose which.
If Supply is the constraint
Traffic exists on the platform but there isn't enough reach for advertisers to buy against. Ad requests are low. Impressions available are low. → See Supply section.
If Demand is the constraint
Traffic exists but advertisers aren't buying or aren't able to. Could be motivation (low ROAS, low CTR) or ability (low campaigns, idle budgets). → See Demand section.
Supply Is there enough traffic for ads to run against?
Supply is the attention base — the searches and page views that create ad opportunities. The drill path starts at platform traffic, then narrows to what FCC actually sees (ad requests), then to how much of that is being filled.
Platform traffic — out of scope for now: Upstream supply metrics (total search volume, page views, sessions, active users on Nahdi) are not currently trackable by FCC. These are owned by Nahdi. Ideally these would be visible in this dashboard to give full supply context — flagged as a data access dependency to resolve.
Ad Requests — what FCC sees
Metric What it tells you If it drops, drill into
Total ad requests
PLA + Display combined
 Volume of ad opportunities reaching FCC. Proxy for platform supply when upstream traffic data isn't available. Split by product type below
Ad requests — PLA
Product Listing Ads (CPC)
 Search-driven ad requests. A drop here means fewer searches are triggering PLA calls — either search volume dropped or the integration broke. Check platform search volume (Nahdi-side); check serving error rate (technical tab)
Ad requests — Display
Display Ads (CPM)
 Page-view-driven ad requests. A drop here means fewer page views are generating display impressions opportunities. Check platform page view volume (Nahdi-side); check display placement config
Fill Rate
Ads served / Total ad requests
 Of the requests FCC receives, how many result in a served ad. The bridge between supply and demand — low fill rate means either no matching campaign (demand gap) or pipeline failure (technical). Industry benchmark: ~54% outside top-tier RMNs. If demand-side gap → Demand section. If pipeline failure → Serving Eval tab (technical)
Demand Are advertisers buying, and are they able to?
Demand is driven by advertiser behaviour — whether they're motivated to spend and whether they're set up to do so. A drop in demand is either a motivation problem (ads aren't delivering value) or an ability problem (advertisers can't configure or scale campaigns). Diagnose which before acting.
Motivation Are advertisers getting value from their spend?
Motivation metrics answer whether the ads are actually working for advertisers. If motivation is low, advertisers reduce budgets or stop campaigning. The drill path follows the value chain — from top-line return down to the individual components that drive it.
Motivation Metrics — drill top-down
Metric What it tells you If it drops, drill into
ROAS
Revenue attributed to ad clicks / Ad spend
 Top-line return for advertisers. The primary motivation signal — if ROAS is strong, advertisers increase budgets. If weak, they pull back. CVR or Clicks dropping?
Incremental Sales
Sales caused by the ad, not organic
 True ad-driven revenue lift above what would have happened without the ad. Stronger motivation signal than ROAS — proves causality, not just correlation. Methodology TBD. Requires holdout measurement — deferred
CVR (Conversion Rate)
Orders / Clicks
 Are clicks leading to purchases? A drop here with stable CTR means the product or landing experience isn't converting — advertiser-side issue. Product-query relevance, landing page, pricing
CTR (Click-Through Rate)
Clicks / Impressions served
 Are users engaging with the ads shown? A drop here means the served ad isn't relevant or compelling enough for users to act. Impressions stable? → relevance problem. Impressions dropped? → fill rate / supply problem
Total Clicks
PLA + Display
 Raw click volume. A drop here can come from fewer impressions (supply) or lower CTR (relevance). Decompose before concluding. Impressions dropped → supply. CTR dropped → relevance (Serving Eval tab)
Total Impressions Served
PLA + Display
 Volume of ads actually shown to users. Function of fill rate × ad requests. A drop here is either supply falling or fill rate falling. Ad requests dropped → supply. Fill rate dropped → demand gap or pipeline
Share of Voice
Advertiser's impressions / Total impressions available
 How much of the available ad inventory a given advertiser is capturing. A drop signals increasing competition or budget exhaustion. Relevant per-advertiser, not platform-wide. Budget exhausted early? → Ability section. More competitors? → competitive context
Ability Are advertisers set up to spend effectively?
Even motivated advertisers can't spend if they're not set up correctly — too few campaigns, exhausted budgets, sparse keyword coverage, or a product that's hard to manage. Ability metrics identify structural gaps in advertiser readiness. A healthy motivation score with weak ability metrics means the platform is leaving money on the table.
Ability Metrics
Metric What it tells you If it drops / is low
Active advertisers
Brands with at least one live campaign
 Breadth of demand. If this is low or shrinking, the revenue base is fragile — too concentrated in too few advertisers. Onboarding gap or churn — check new advertiser additions vs. deactivations
Active campaigns
Campaigns in live / running state
 Depth of demand. An advertiser with one campaign is fragile. More campaigns across more categories = more robust demand. Paused or ended campaigns — check budget exhaustion rate
Active keywords
Keywords triggered at least once in last 7 days
 How much of the configured keyword inventory is actually live. Idle keywords reduce coverage and fill rate. High idle rate → keyword-query mismatch or low search volume for those terms
Total keywords configured
Across all live campaigns
 Coverage of demand. Low keyword count relative to search volume = large share of queries with no campaign match. Compare against top search queries — identify uncovered head terms
Total listings in campaigns
Unique products across all live campaigns
 Product coverage in ads. Low listing count = narrow advertiser participation or sparse catalogue coverage. Check % of Nahdi catalogue covered by at least one campaign
Budget utilisation rate
Spend / Configured budget
 Are advertisers spending their full allocated budget? Low utilisation = bids too low or keywords not matching. High utilisation early in the period = budget caps are limiting spend. Low → bid or keyword gap. High with early exhaustion → budget cap too tight
Time to set up a campaign
Median time from creation to first impression
 Ease of onboarding. A long setup time signals product friction that's suppressing demand before it even starts. Review onboarding flow for drop-off points
Time to edit / manage a campaign
Median time for common management actions
 Ease of ongoing use. High management time discourages active optimisation, which leads to idle budgets and stale keywords. Review campaign management flows for friction
Reading motivation and ability together: Motivation dropping with ability stable → the product isn't delivering returns, focus on relevance and conversion. Ability dropping with motivation stable → advertisers want to spend but can't, focus on onboarding and campaign tooling. Both dropping → structural demand problem, needs coordinated fix.
