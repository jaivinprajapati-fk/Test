Sanity Check — Banner Group Coverage
Are the 140 Active BGs Actually Serving?
For the 140 BGs that have products, we check: when their configured keywords are searched, do the products come back? This is the most basic measure of whether a banner group is working.

Issue: 17 banner groups have products configured but none of them come back for any keyword. That's 141 product slots that have zero chance of being served today. Root cause is unknown at this stage — could be catalogue sync, Solr indexing, or keyword setup. This needs to be the first engineering deep dive.
Note on the 7 partial BGs: Some products within these BGs aren't reachable via any of their keywords. This is a secondary gap — the BG works for some products but not all. Needs investigation after the 17 zero-coverage BGs are resolved.

Coverage definition: for each BG, we search with each of its configured keywords and check how many of its products appear in results. 100% = all products appeared for at least one keyword. 0% = no product appeared for any keyword.
